SPANDANA EMBROIDERY
Care for embroidered clothes

A small, general-purpose care note

1. Check the garment's care label first. Fabric, lining, dye and construction all matter.
2. When suitable for the garment, turn it inside out and hand wash gently in cool water.
3. Do not wring or scrub directly over the embroidery. Press water out gently with a clean towel.
4. Dry flat or as directed by the garment label, away from harsh direct sunlight.
5. If ironing is suitable, turn the garment inside out and use a gentle setting with a pressing cloth.
6. For delicate, valuable or uncertain pieces, ask a garment-care professional before washing.

This included project guide is general advice, not a guarantee for every fabric or stitch.
Always follow the garment label and seek professional care where appropriate.
