## 7. Accessibility requirements

Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices as far as possible.

- Use semantic HTML and landmarks such as header, nav, main, and footer.
- Provide a skip link, logical heading hierarchy, descriptive page titles, and meaningful link text.
- Ensure every form field has a visible label, instructions where needed, and accessible validation feedback.

- Give meaningful images useful alt text; use empty alt text for purely decorative images.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate status, price tier, or errors.
- Check text and interactive-element contrast in both light and dark modes.

- Ensure animations do not cause essential content to disappear or become inaccessible.

- Keep effects lightweight, avoid unnecessary re-renders, and clean up event listeners and observers.
